Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 June 2025

1. Who We Are

SokoLeo is operated by Papuza Agri-Tech Limited, a company registered in Kenya, trading under the name SokoLeo. We run an agricultural marketplace that connects farmers and traders across Kenya.

For any data-related enquiries, contact us at aligwetu@gmail.com or via WhatsApp through the admin contact listed in the app.

2. What Data We Collect

When you use SokoLeo, we may collect the following personal data:

  • Identity data: Your full name and phone number
  • Verification data: National ID number (used for identity verification only — see Section 5 on data minimisation)
  • Location data: County, town, and general area
  • Marketplace data: Produce listings you create, transaction history
  • Payment references: M-PESA transaction reference numbers (not card or bank details)

3. Why We Collect It

We collect personal data only for the following purposes:

  • Account registration and authentication via OTP
  • Identity verification to maintain a trusted marketplace
  • Enabling produce listings and trade transactions between farmers and traders
  • Processing escrow payments via M-PESA
  • Sending SMS notifications (via Africa's Talking) and WhatsApp messages (via Twilio) about your orders, trades, and account

4. Data Minimisation — National ID

Your National ID number is collected solely to verify your identity. Once an admin reviews and approves or rejects your verification:

  • Only the last 4 digits of your ID number are retained as a masked reference
  • Any uploaded ID photo is permanently deleted from our storage
  • Your full ID number is not stored

This minimisation is consistent with the data minimisation principle under Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019.

5. Your Rights

Under the Data Protection Act 2019, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Delete your account and associated personal data
  • Object to the processing of your data in certain circumstances

To exercise any of these rights, contact us via WhatsApp through the admin contact in the app, or email aligwetu@gmail.com. We will respond within 21 days.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to any third party. Data is shared only where strictly necessary to deliver the service:

  • Africa's Talking — for sending SMS OTP codes and notifications
  • Twilio — for WhatsApp notifications and customer support messages
  • Safaricom / M-PESA — for processing escrow payments and confirming transactions

Each of these providers is bound by their own data processing terms and relevant data protection obligations.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect your data, including:

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for all data in transit
  • Parameterised database queries to prevent SQL injection
  • Access-controlled admin panel requiring a secret admin token
  • Automatic deletion of ID photos after verification

No system is completely secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately.

8. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you request deletion, we will remove your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain records for legal or regulatory purposes.

9. Contact Us

For any questions about this Privacy Policy or to exercise your data rights, contact:

  • Email: aligwetu@gmail.com
  • WhatsApp: via the admin contact in the SokoLeo app
  • Company: Papuza Agri-Tech Limited, Kenya
