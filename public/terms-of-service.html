4. Commission

SokoLeo charges a commission on completed transactions. The applicable commission rate is displayed within the app before you confirm a transaction. Commission is deducted from escrow funds before the net amount is released to the seller.

5. Escrow and Payments

SokoLeo uses an escrow system for marketplace transactions:

  • When a trader confirms a purchase, payment is held in escrow via M-PESA
  • Funds are released to the farmer only after delivery is confirmed through the pickup verification system
  • If delivery does not occur, the dispute resolution process applies (see Section 6)

Payments are processed through Safaricom's M-PESA service. SokoLeo is not a bank or payment institution and does not hold funds directly.

6. Dispute Resolution

If a dispute arises between a farmer and a trader over a transaction:

  • Either party may raise a dispute through the SokoLeo admin contact
  • An SokoLeo admin will mediate and aim to resolve the dispute within 48 hours
  • The admin's decision on the release or refund of escrow funds is final within the platform

SokoLeo's dispute process applies only to transactions conducted through the platform's escrow system. Off-platform transactions are not covered.
